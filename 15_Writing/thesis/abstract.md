# Abstract

> **Version:** `0.1.0` | **Status:** Draft (Pending Human Review) | **Last Updated:** 2026-11-21
> **Writing Unit:** `WR0001`
> **Traceability:** Synthesizes verified findings from `KI0001`, `KI0003`, `KI0027`, `KI0031`, `KI0032`, `KI0035`, `KI0042`–`KI0045`, `EXP0001`–`EXP0006`, `EVAL0001`–`EVAL0007`, `P0003`, `P0007`, `P0016`, `P0018`, `P0023`, `CLAIM0001`, `CLAIM0003`, and `CLAIM0006`.

---

Synthetic human face generation across Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and diffusion models has outpaced standalone black-box detectors, which routinely achieve high in-distribution accuracy yet degrade severely under unseen generators, dataset shift, and lossy post-processing (`WR0001`; `KI0001`; `P0003`; `P0007`; `P0016`; `P0018`; `P0023`; `CLAIM0001`; `CLAIM0003`; `CLAIM0006`). To address both cross-generator brittleness and the lack of auditable forensic workflows, this thesis reframes synthetic face detection around a system-level primary contribution: the **AI Forensic Analyst**, an 18-module evidence-driven forensic platform (`MOD001`–`MOD018`) in which the deep learning detector operates as one isolated, replaceable specialist rather than an autonomous arbiter (`KI0003`; `KI0044`; `DEC0003`).

First, we establish a strictly public-only, open-license benchmark spanning five validated datasets (`DS0001`–`DS0005`: ArtiFact face subset, DiFF official test, 140k Real and Fake Faces, Synthbuster, and FairFace), six versioned preprocessing pipelines (`PP0001`–`PP0006`), and ten leakage-checked grouped and leave-one-generator-out splits (`SPLIT0001`–`SPLIT0002`) (`KI0042`; `DEC0004`; `DEC0005`). Second, we train and rigorously evaluate a standardized ResNet-50 baseline across six controlled GPU experiments (`EXP0001`–`EXP0006`; `EVAL0001`–`EVAL0006`) (`KI0027`; `KI0043`). On `DS0001` `SPLIT0001` test (`n=7,505`) at its validation-frozen threshold (`0.3951`), `MODEL0001` attains ROC-AUC `0.9261`, PR-AUC `0.9348`, balanced accuracy `0.8483`, MCC `0.6978`, and ECE `0.0525`. However, stress testing quantifies severe standalone fragility: in-distribution false negative rates reach `43.7%` on `stylegan3`; fine-detail perturbations (`low_quality_upload`, downscaling, blur, noise, JPEG quality 10) collapse balanced accuracy toward `0.50` by driving false positive rates toward `1.0`; cross-dataset unseen-generator FNR rises to `48.1%` (`50.2%` on `DS0002` DiFF) while external real-face FPR reaches `30.4%` (`DS0003`), `76.7%` (`DS0004`, ROC-AUC `0.5172`), and `82.6%` (`DS0005` FairFace); and controlled `stable_diffusion` holdout (`EXP0002`; `EVAL0002`) drops recall from `96.3%` (`MODEL0001`, seen) to `13.7%` (`MODEL0008`, unseen) on the identical 299 test images (`15.2%` across all 1,923 held-out images) (`KI0031`; `KI0032`). One-factor ablations (`EXP0003`–`EXP0006`) confirm that ImageNet pretraining (`-0.2243` ROC-AUC), 224-pixel resolution (`-0.0376`), and augmentation (`-0.0336`) all exceed the seed-repeatability noise floor (`+0.0011`), reported strictly as saved-run differences with `superiority_claim=none`.

Third, we implement and validate the 18-module AI Forensic Analyst (`11_AI_Forensic_System/`), separating upload intake (`MOD001`), image validation (`MOD002`), nine independent non-detector collectors (`MOD003`), isolated deep learning inference (`MOD004`), an append-only `EV` registry (`MOD005`), non-destructive reliability grading (`MOD006`), replaceable rule-based fusion (`MOD007`; `DEC0009`), four-dimensional validation-calibrated confidence estimation (`MOD009`; `DEC0010`), an image-blind four-way Decision Engine (`MOD008`: `Real`, `Fake`, `Inconclusive`, `Unknown`), Policy A.9 data-only explainability (`MOD010`), programmatically `EV`-verified reasoning (`MOD011`), Template 24 provenance-stamped reporting (`MOD012`), audit logging (`MOD015`), structured failure handling across eight canonical modes (`MOD016`), and full-stack web delivery via a seven-app Django REST API (`MOD013`) and a 14-page, 24-component React application (`MOD014`) (`KI0044`; `KI0045`). In Phase E14 whole-platform evaluation (`EVAL0007`), the system passes all 9 quality dimensions and all 16 end-to-end scenarios (`135` verified reasoning sentences, `0` ungrounded leaks, mean latency `60.02 ms`), demonstrating that unusable supporting evidence overrides a `0.99` detector score into an explicit `Inconclusive` verdict while missing or timed-out specialists degrade cleanly to `Unknown` with `null` confidences (`KI0035`). All 85 automated unit/API/frontend tests, live cross-tier integration tests, six automated UAT scenarios, and ten quality gates (Checklists 2–12) pass, establishing an honest, reproducible, and modality-extensible foundation for image-based digital forensics (`WR0001`).

**Keywords:** AI-generated face detection, digital forensics, cross-generator generalization, evidence fusion, confidence calibration, explainable forensic reporting, GANs, diffusion models (`WR0001`; `KI0001`; `KI0044`).
