# Decision: DEC0003

**Date:** 2026-07-31

**Problem:** Select the primary research direction for the AI Digital Forensics project (Version 1: image-based AI-generated face detection). The direction must align with the project's primary scientific contribution — the AI Forensic Analyst (§1.2) — and be grounded in the identified research gaps (GAP0001–GAP0008) and verified claims (CLAIM0001–CLAIM0008).

**Options:**

1. **Direction 1 — Evidence-Driven Forensic Analysis System for Cross-Generator Synthetic Face Detection:** Build the full AI Forensic Analyst as a multi-evidence forensic system fusing spatial, spectral, and semantic evidence streams. Addresses GAP0001 (Critical), GAP0003 (Critical), GAP0007 (High). Novelty: ★★★★★. Complexity: High. Primary contribution alignment: Perfect.

2. **Direction 2 — Hybrid Spatial-Frequency Feature Fusion:** Dual-branch neural architecture fusing CNN/ViT spatial features with frequency domain representations. Addresses GAP0001, GAP0007, GAP0004. Novelty: ★★★☆☆. Complexity: Medium. Primary contribution alignment: Partial (one evidence collector only).

3. **Direction 3 — Bias-Aware Cross-Generator Detection with Dataset Integrity Auditing:** Detection framework with explicit dataset bias auditing and mitigation. Addresses GAP0002, GAP0001. Novelty: ★★★★☆. Complexity: Medium. Primary contribution alignment: Weak.

4. **Direction 4 — Hierarchical Source Attribution with Forensic Reporting:** Multi-level classifier (real/fake → GAN/diffusion → specific generator) with reporting. Addresses GAP0005, GAP0001, GAP0003. Novelty: ★★☆☆☆ (P0018 exists). Complexity: Medium-High. Primary contribution alignment: Moderate.

5. **Direction 5 — Compression-Robust Synthetic Face Detection for Social Media Forensics:** Pipeline optimized for social-media-degraded synthetic faces. Addresses GAP0004, GAP0001. Novelty: ★★★☆☆. Complexity: Medium. Primary contribution alignment: Weak.

**Chosen Option:** **Direction 1 — Evidence-Driven Forensic Analysis System for Cross-Generator Synthetic Face Detection.** Build the full AI Forensic Analyst as a multi-evidence forensic system fusing spatial, spectral, and semantic evidence streams to detect synthetic faces regardless of generator architecture (GAN or diffusion). Addresses GAP0001 (Critical), GAP0003 (Critical), GAP0007 (High).

**Rejected Options:**
- Direction 2 (Hybrid Spatial-Frequency Feature Fusion) — reduces the project to a single detector; partial alignment with primary contribution. Will be subsumed as one evidence collector within Direction 1.
- Direction 3 (Bias-Aware Detection with Dataset Auditing) — primarily a methodology contribution; weak alignment with primary contribution. Bias-aware evaluation will be integrated into Direction 1's evaluation protocol instead.
- Direction 4 (Hierarchical Source Attribution) — low novelty due to existing work (P0018); moderate alignment. Hierarchical reasoning will inform the decision engine design within Direction 1.
- Direction 5 (Compression-Robust Detection) — narrow scope; weak alignment. Robustness strategies will be integrated as a training component within Direction 1's evidence collectors.

**Reason:** Direction 1 is the only candidate that directly builds the AI Forensic Analyst — the project's declared primary scientific contribution (§1.2). It has the highest novelty (no existing work combines multiple independent evidence collectors with validation, fusion, confidence calibration, and forensic reporting for synthetic face detection), the best publication potential, perfect alignment with the project identity, and excellent future scalability (new modalities become new evidence collectors). Directions 2–5 are each subsumed as components within Direction 1, meaning no value is lost. The higher implementation complexity is managed by the project's modular architecture where each module is independently built, tested, and integrated.

**Expected Impact:** (1) A novel system-level contribution differentiating this thesis from classifier-only work. (2) Strong publication potential at multimedia/forensics venues. (3) A reusable forensic platform extensible to audio, video, and text modalities in future versions. (4) Cross-generator generalization addressing the most critical gap in the field (GAP0001, supported by 11 papers). (5) Explainable forensic reports addressing the second most critical gap (GAP0003, supported by 9 papers).

**Supporting Evidence:**
- Research Gap Registry: GAP0001–GAP0008 (see `02_Literature/research_gap/research_gap.csv`)
- Claim Database: CLAIM0001–CLAIM0008 (see `02_Literature/claims/claim_database.csv`)
- Papers Registry: P0001–P0028 (see `02_Literature/metadata/papers.csv`)
- Full direction analysis: `02_Literature/research_directions.md`

**Future Review:** Review at the end of the dataset phase (STEP-024 gate) to confirm the direction remains viable given the available datasets. Also review at the model foundation phase (STEP-034) to confirm backbone and fusion strategy choices align with the chosen direction.
