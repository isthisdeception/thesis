# Decision: DEC0010

**Decision Status:** APPROVED (Human, 2026-11-18)
**Date:** 2026-11-18

---

**Problem:** Modules 8 and 9 require (1) a documented, E4-calibrated formula for the four confidence numbers (`prediction_confidence`, `evidence_confidence`, `system_confidence`, `decision_confidence`) that never equates raw softmax $P(\text{fake})$ with forensic certainty, and (2) an explicit rule table mapping validated fused evidence + validated confidence onto `Real`, `Fake`, `Inconclusive`, and `Unknown`.

---

**Options:**

| Component | Approved choice (`e4_calibrated_v1` / `MOD008` `0.1.0`) | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| `prediction_confidence` | Look up predicted-class raw confidence ($p$ if $p \ge t$ else $1-p$) in `08_Evaluation/EVAL0001/confidence_distribution.csv` (`split=val`) to obtain empirical validation accuracy, scaled by MOD006 reliability weight (`high=1.0`, `medium=0.6`, `low=0.3`); `null` if no usable detector item | Raw $P(\text{fake})$ or uncalibrated $\max(p, 1-p)$ (rejected: violates Module 9 and E4) |
| `evidence_confidence` | Mean MOD006 reliability weight across validated collector items (`unusable=0.0`), multiplied by `0.5` if validation flags conflicting evidence | Ignore unusable items (rejected: hides failed checks) |
| `system_confidence` | Usable validated collector items divided by total collector attempts + refused ids + prior MOD016 case failures | Fixed 1.0 when decision runs (rejected: hides collector failures) |
| `decision_confidence` | `0.5 * prediction + 0.3 * evidence + 0.2 * system`, capped by `min(evidence, system)` when either is $\le 0.5$ or no non-detector evidence is usable, and discounted by `0.85` (single-source) and `0.5` (contested) | Copy `prediction_confidence` (rejected: violates MOD009 spec test) |
| `MOD008` decision mapping | `no_directional_evidence` → `Unknown`; `contested`, validation conflict, or `decision_confidence < 0.50` → `Inconclusive` (+ `low_confidence` warning); otherwise `positive_class` → `Fake`, `negative_class` → `Real` | Binary `Fake`/`Real` only (rejected: hides uncertainty) |

---

**Chosen Option:** Implement `e4_calibrated_v1` in `11_AI_Forensic_System/confidence/confidence_config.json` and the 4-way decision rules in `11_AI_Forensic_System/decision/decision_config.json`.

**Rejected Options:** Using raw softmax as confidence; omitting `Inconclusive` or `Unknown`; computing calibration on the `test` split (violates §7.2 validation-only selection policy).

---

**Reason for Chosen Option:** Uses only `val`-split calibration artifacts from EVAL0001, keeps source collector confidences untouched, guarantees `prediction_confidence != decision_confidence` when supporting evidence is unusable or single-source, and makes `Inconclusive` and `Unknown` explicit, auditable outcomes.

---

**Expected Impact:**
- When `MODEL0001` emits $p = 0.99$, `prediction_confidence` is `0.971745` (`val` bin `[0.95, 1.0]` accuracy), never `0.99`.
- When all other collectors are `unusable`, `decision_confidence` drops to $\le 0.25$ and MOD008 outputs `Inconclusive` with a `low_confidence` warning record.
- When `MODEL0001` is unavailable, `prediction_confidence` and `decision_confidence` stay `null` and MOD008 outputs `Unknown`.

---

**Supporting Evidence:** `11_AI_Forensic_System/confidence/`; `11_AI_Forensic_System/decision/`; `11_AI_Forensic_System/tests/test_decision_confidence.py`; `08_Evaluation/EVAL0001/calibration_threshold.md`; `08_Evaluation/EVAL0001/confidence_distribution.csv`.

---

**Future Review:** Revisit calibration table pointer if a new model evaluation (`EVAL0003+`) supersedes `EVAL0001`.
