# Decision: DEC0006

**Decision Status:** APPROVED & LOCKED (Human Ratified)
**Date:** 2026-11-07

---

**Problem:** Checklist 5 requires expected metrics before training. The values in `EXP0001/config.yaml` (`expected_outcome`) were proposed by the AI in STEP-035 and needed human approval, because the config becomes immutable once training starts.

---

**Options Evaluated:**

| Option | Expected outcome | Notes |
|--------|------------------|-------|
| A | SPLIT0001 test: ROC-AUC ≥ 0.95, balanced accuracy ≥ 0.90 at the validation-chosen threshold; ROC-AUC < 0.90 triggers a pipeline/data investigation before comparators | Sanity expectations for an in-distribution baseline (test generators seen in training) |
| B | No numeric expectation | Fails Checklist 5 ("expected metrics") |

---

**Chosen Option:** **Option A**, as written in `06_Experiments/EXP0001/config.yaml`.

**Rejected Options:** Option B, because Checklist 5 requires expected metrics before training.

---

**Reason for Chosen Option:** SPLIT0001 is an in-distribution, class-balanced split (all 13 generators appear in train, val, and test), so a pretrained ResNet-50 should separate the classes well. A result far below these values more likely indicates a pipeline or data defect than a model limitation. The thresholds are sanity checks, not targets to tune toward; cross-generator generalization is assessed on the SPLIT0002 hold-out track.

---

**Expected Impact:**
- EXP0001 may launch once the LR Finder value and launch-time `git_commit` / `timestamp` are recorded (STEP-039).
- If test ROC-AUC < 0.90, MODEL0002 / MODEL0003 comparator runs wait until a failure analysis (Phase M14) is complete.
- Missing the thresholds does not invalidate the run; the result is reported as observed.

---

**Supporting Evidence:**

- `05_Models/baseline_plan.md` (baseline definition, threshold policy)
