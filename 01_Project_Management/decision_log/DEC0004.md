# Decision: DEC0004

**Decision Status:** APPROVED & LOCKED (Human Ratified)  
**DEF-003 Resolution:** RESOLVED (Public-Only Dataset Strategy)  
**Date:** 2026-08-01  

---

**Problem:** Select the primary training and evaluation datasets for the Evidence-Driven Forensic Analysis System for Cross-Generator Synthetic Face Detection (Direction 1, DEC0003). This resolves deferred decision **DEF-003** ("Primary training dataset(s)") which is blocking for the training gate.

Per the human mandate, the project enforces a **Strict Public-Only Dataset Policy**: only datasets with open, public, zero-restriction availability and permissive licenses (MIT, CC BY, CC BY-NC) are selected. Restricted access, broken links, or approval-gated academic forms (e.g., Guarnera MFD) are excluded to ensure 100% open reproducibility, zero execution friction, and instant Kaggle/GitHub integration.

**Options Evaluated:**

All 17 candidates from `dataset_candidates.csv` were scored on 16 Phase D2 dimensions (see `dataset_evaluation.csv`).

---

**Chosen Option:** **Public-Only Multi-Dataset Strategy** with designated roles:

### 1. Primary Training Dataset: **CAND0017 — Artifact Dataset (Face Subset)** (`PRIMARY-TRAIN`)
- **Access & License:** 100% Public on HuggingFace / OpenML under the **MIT License**. Zero permission friction.
- **Generator Coverage:** 13 generators (ProGAN, StyleGAN2, StyleGAN3, Stable Diffusion v1.4/v1.5/v2.1, Midjourney, VQKD, etc.).
- **Subsampling & Handling Strategy:** The full Artifact dataset is 2.5M images across 25 categories. To maintain low storage overhead and fast Kaggle GPU training, we extract the **Face Modality Subset** and apply deterministic stratified sampling (Handbook §4 Phase D12):
  - **Sampled Size:** 50,000 images total (~25,000 real faces + ~25,000 synthetic faces evenly distributed across the 13 generators at ~2,000 images per generator).
  - **Storage Footprint:** ~5–8 GB (easily fits in Kaggle RAM/GPU and local disk).

### 2. Primary Evaluation Dataset: **CAND0001 — DiFF (Diffusion Facial Forgery Dataset)** (`PRIMARY-EVAL`)
- **Access & License:** Publicly hosted on GitHub / Drive (ACM MM 2024) under CC BY-NC 4.0.
- **Role:** Serves as the primary diffusion-era evaluation benchmark for testing cross-generator generalization (E9 unseen-generator protocol). It includes advanced diffusion models (DALL-E 2, DALL-E 3, IF, LDM) not evaluated in standard GAN benchmarks.

### 3. Quick Baseline Dataset (For Board Presentation): **CAND0010 — Real and Fake Face Detection (140k)** (`QUICK-BASELINE`)
- **Access & License:** Hosted directly on Kaggle Datasets (Instant 1-click access).
- **Role:** Used exclusively for training an immediate ResNet-34 baseline classifier to present baseline metrics to the board, proving project workflow execution prior to full system integration.

### 4. Supplementary Public Datasets:
- **CAND0002 — Synthbuster Dataset** (`SUPPLEMENTARY-FREQUENCY`): Public on Zenodo (CC BY 4.0). Used for frequency-domain evidence collector validation due to its uncompressed RAISE real image baselines.
- **CAND0009 — AI-Face-FairnessBench** (`SUPPLEMENTARY-BIAS`): Public on HuggingFace (MIT License). Used for evaluating demographic fairness and bias reporting in forensic analyst outputs (GAP0002).

---

**Rejected Options:**

- **CAND0012 (Guarnera MFD)** — Excluded due to restricted access forms and non-functional web host (`iplab.dmi.unict.it`). Violates the Public-Only mandate. Replaced by CAND0017 (Artifact Face Subset).
- **CAND0003 (FF++), CAND0004 (Celeb-DF v2), CAND0005 (DFDC), CAND0013 (FakeAVCeleb)** — Excluded because they focus on video face manipulation/swapping (Deepfakes, FaceSwap), whereas V1 scope (§1.2) is image-based AI face generation detection. Additionally, FF++ requires gated academic forms.
- **CAND0011 (Augmented Real and Fake)** — Flagged as a duplicate 2k-image subset of CAND0010. Rejected to eliminate redundant data.
- **CAND0014 (CIFAKE)** — Rejected due to low resolution (32×32 CIFAR format), which prevents spatial and frequency forensic artifact analysis.

---

**Reason for Chosen Option:**

1. **100% Public & Frictionless:** Zero waiting for email approvals, broken site links, or gated forms. Every dataset is immediately downloadable via HuggingFace, Zenodo, GitHub, or Kaggle.
2. **Open Science & MIT License:** CAND0017 (Artifact) and CAND0009 (FairnessBench) carry MIT licenses, maximizing scientific reproducibility and open-source dissemination.
3. **Cross-Generator Generalization (GAP0001):** Subsampling Artifact (13 generators) for training and evaluating on DiFF (13 diffusion methods) creates a rigorous E9 unseen-generator test protocol.
4. **Feasible & Scalable:** Subsampling Artifact down to a 50k face subset ensures fast training (under 1 hour per baseline on Kaggle GPU) while preserving statistical validity.
5. **Immediate Board Deliverable:** CAND0010 provides instant Kaggle execution for running a ResNet-34 baseline model for the upcoming board meeting.
