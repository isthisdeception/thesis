# Decision: DEC0009

**Decision Status:** APPROVED (Human, 2026-11-18)
**Date:** 2026-11-18

---

**Problem:** Module 7 needs a first fusion strategy. DEF-004 defers the choice between rule-based, weighted, and Bayesian fusion, with rule-based first. The architecture (STEP-051) left the rule table for a later step and human approval. Only one evidence type today has a direction (the detector probability against its frozen threshold); the other collectors record measurements with no agreed direction.

---

**Options:**

| Option | What it needs | Status now |
|---|---|---|
| Rule-based, reliability-weighted | MOD006 quality labels, the recorded threshold | possible |
| Weighted (learned weights) | labelled cases with every collector's output | no such data yet |
| Bayesian | per-collector likelihoods, calibrated | only the detector is calibrated (E4) |

Rule table choices:

| Parameter | Approved value | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Reliability weights | high 1.0, medium 0.6, low 0.3, unusable never weighted | equal weights (rejected: Module 6 forbids uniform reliability) |
| Directional rule | `deep_learning_prediction`: probability ≥ recorded threshold (0.395 for MODEL0001) → positive-class side | 0.5 cut (rejected: ignores the E4 frozen threshold) |
| Non-directional types | context: cited, no vote | assign directions to measurements (rejected: no evidence for a direction) |
| Contested band | both sides present and \|balance\| < 0.5 | any disagreement is contested |

---

**Chosen Option:** `rule_based` strategy with the table above (`11_AI_Forensic_System/fusion/fusion_config.json`, `table_version` 0.1.0). `weighted` and `bayesian` stay reserved ids on the same `FusionStrategy` interface and are not registered.

**Rejected Options:** Weighted and Bayesian fusion now (no data to fit or calibrate them for collectors other than the detector).

---

**Reason for Chosen Option:** It is interpretable: every contribution is a cited `EV` id with its weight and side. It honours Module 6 (reliability is not uniform) and E4 (the frozen threshold, not 0.5). It makes no claim for collectors whose direction is unknown.

---

**Expected Impact:**
- With only MODEL0001 the lean is single-source; MOD006 marks such fused items `medium`. MOD008 (STEP-057) must decide what a single-source lean allows.
- The detector's known weaknesses (STEP-046 robustness, STEP-047 cross-dataset FPR) carry straight into the lean until other collectors gain directional rules.
- Changing a weight or band is a config change with a new `table_version`; no code change.

---

**Supporting Evidence:** `11_AI_Forensic_System/fusion/`; `11_AI_Forensic_System/tests/test_fusion.py`; `06_Experiments/EXP0001/metrics/threshold.json`; `08_Evaluation/EVAL0001/` (calibration, ECE 0.052).

---

**Future Review:** After STEP-059 (forensic gate) and once labelled case data exists, compare a `weighted` strategy against this baseline; add directional rules for collectors with measured discriminative power.
