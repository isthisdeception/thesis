# DEC0001: Open-Source License Selection

**Decision ID:** DEC0001
**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Problem:** The undergraduate research project requires an open-source license that maximizes reproducibility and frictionless adoption while providing a clear legal framework.
**Options:**
1. MIT License
2. Apache License 2.0
**Chosen Option:** MIT License
**Rejected Options:** Apache License 2.0
**Reason:** The MIT License is the most widely recognized and permissive license, offering the lowest barrier to entry. For an undergraduate project, maximizing visibility and frictionless adoption by the academic community is preferred over the explicit patent protections offered by Apache 2.0, which add unnecessary complexity.
**Expected Impact:** Source code can be easily reused and adapted by other researchers; highest probability of academic ecosystem adoption.
**Supporting Evidence:** The MIT license is a prevailing standard for open-source AI and academic projects focused on rapid, unencumbered dissemination.
**Future Review:** Re-evaluate if the project is commercialized, spun out into a startup, or develops patentable architectures.
