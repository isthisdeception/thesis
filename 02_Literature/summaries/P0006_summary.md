## Problem
Diffusion models generate increasingly realistic face images, and existing detection methods (designed for GANs) may not capture diffusion-specific artifacts. Novel detection features specific to the diffusion generation process are needed.

## Motivation
The diffusion process involves iterative denoising from noise to image. By attempting to reconstruct an image through the diffusion process and comparing the reconstruction with the original, the reconstruction error may reveal whether the image was originally generated by a diffusion model — a model-specific forensic signal.

## Method
Extract **diffusion reconstruction error features** by: (1) encoding the input image into the diffusion latent space, (2) attempting to reconstruct it via the diffusion denoising process, (3) comparing the reconstruction with the original to extract error features that discriminate between real and diffusion-generated images.

## Architecture
Diffusion model-based feature extraction (reconstruction error) + classifier. Uses a diffusion model as a feature extractor rather than a traditional CNN.
