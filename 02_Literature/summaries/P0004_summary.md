## Problem
Distributed face recognition systems face security and privacy challenges. Combining blockchain for data integrity with GANs for privacy-preserving face synthesis could address both concerns.

## Motivation
Protecting facial data privacy while maintaining recognition accuracy requires generating synthetic faces that preserve identity characteristics without exposing real biometric data.

## Method
GAN-based face generation for privacy enhancement combined with blockchain for data integrity in distributed face recognition systems.

## Architecture
