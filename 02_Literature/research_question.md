# Research Question

**Primary Question:** How can we improve the cross-generator generalization of image-based AI face detection models to maintain high accuracy on unseen generative architectures?

## Sub-questions
1. What are the common forensic artifacts shared across different generative architectures (e.g., GANs, diffusion models) in face images?
2. How do current state-of-the-art detection models degrade when tested on novel generators?
3. Which regularization or data augmentation strategies most effectively mitigate overfitting to specific generators?
