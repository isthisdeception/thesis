# Research Gap: GAP0004 — Degradation of Forensic Artifacts Under Real-World Compression & Post-Processing

**Category:** Robustness & Anti-Forensics  
**Supporting Papers:** P0003, P0005, P0010, P0013, P0016, P0018  
**Frequency:** 6  
**Conflicting Papers:** None  
**Importance:** High  
**Potential Contribution:** Robust feature extraction pipelines combining spatial features with noise-invariant frequency transforms and data augmentation strategies specifically targeted at social media re-compression channels.  
**Implementation Difficulty:** Medium  
**Publication Potential:** High  

---

## Overview & Description

High-frequency spectral artifacts (e.g., DCT high-band anomalies, grid noise) and subtle spatial gradient cues degrade rapidly when images undergo lossy JPEG compression, spatial downscaling, noise injection, or re-encoding during transmission across social media platforms (e.g., WhatsApp, Twitter/X, Instagram). Detectors that achieve 99%+ accuracy on pristine laboratory datasets experience sharp performance degradation in real-world scenarios.

### Research Opportunity & Forensic Impact
Developing robust detection techniques and compression-aware training augmentations that preserve diagnostic forensic signal under heavy post-processing and multi-stage re-compression.
