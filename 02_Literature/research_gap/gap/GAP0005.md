# Research Gap: GAP0005 — Absence of Multi-Class Source Attribution Across Generator Families

**Category:** Source Attribution & Fine-Grained Forensics  
**Supporting Papers:** P0018, P0021, P0023  
**Frequency:** 3  
**Conflicting Papers:** None  
**Importance:** High  
**Potential Contribution:** Fine-grained multiclass attribution taxonomy and hierarchical classifier capable of distinguishing between specific generator architectures (e.g., StyleGAN2 vs. StyleGAN3 vs. Stable Diffusion v1.5 vs. SDXL vs. Midjourney v5).  
**Implementation Difficulty:** Medium  
**Publication Potential:** High  

---

## Overview & Description

Binary classification (Real vs. Fake) provides limited intelligence for digital investigations. Determining the specific generator family or model architecture responsible for producing a synthetic image is vital for threat intelligence, tracking malicious campaigns, and legal accountability. However, multi-class attribution methods covering both GAN and diffusion model families simultaneously remain poorly developed and unstandardized.

### Research Opportunity & Forensic Impact
Formulating a hierarchical multi-class forensic attribution engine capable of first classifying image authenticity, then identifying the generative paradigm (GAN vs. Diffusion), and finally pin-pointing the specific model architecture.
