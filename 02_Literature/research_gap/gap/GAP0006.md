# Research Gap: GAP0006 — Vulnerability to Anti-Forensic Attacks and Fingerprint Removal

**Category:** Adversarial Robustness & Anti-Forensics  
**Supporting Papers:** P0008, P0019  
**Frequency:** 2  
**Conflicting Papers:** None  
**Importance:** Medium  
**Potential Contribution:** Defense mechanisms against anti-forensic fingerprint perturbations and adversarial attacks designed to obscure synthetic image signatures.  
**Implementation Difficulty:** Hard  
**Publication Potential:** High  

---

## Overview & Description

Adversaries can apply post-processing operations, latent space filtering, or targeted anti-forensic perturbations to intentionally scrub known GAN/Diffusion spectral fingerprints while preserving visual quality. Detector robustness against deliberate adversarial anti-forensic attacks is largely unassessed in current literature.

### Research Opportunity & Forensic Impact
Evaluating and strengthening detector resilience against anti-forensic attacks through adversarial training, feature masking, and multi-domain redundancy.
