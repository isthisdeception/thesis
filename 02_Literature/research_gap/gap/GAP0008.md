# Research Gap: GAP0008 — Out-of-Date Literature Surveys Lacking Diffusion Era Synthesis

**Category:** Literature Synthesis  
**Supporting Papers:** P0022, P0024, P0026, P0027, P0028  
**Frequency:** 5  
**Conflicting Papers:** None  
**Importance:** Medium  
**Potential Contribution:** Comprehensive, up-to-date literature review and taxonomy bridging the gap between legacy GAN detection literature and emerging diffusion model detection paradigms.  
**Implementation Difficulty:** Easy  
**Publication Potential:** Medium  

---

## Overview & Description

Highly-cited deepfake literature surveys (such as those from 2020-2023) focus almost exclusively on GAN-generated faces and deepfake video manipulation. The rapid rise of latent diffusion models (2023–2026) has created a significant synthesis gap in the literature regarding unified taxonomies, comparative benchmarks, and evaluation methodologies across both paradigm eras.

### Research Opportunity & Forensic Impact
Synthesizing a comprehensive, living literature survey covering the evolution of synthetic face detection from GAN convolutional traces to diffusion reconstruction error and foundation models.
