## Overview & Description

Standalone deepfake detectors typically issue uncalibrated binary decisions using a fixed probability cutoff (such as 0.5). Under unseen diffusion generators, counter-forensic post-processing, or cross-dataset shifts, raw softmax outputs become overconfident or undergo severe score-distribution shift (e.g., `DDPM` retaining 90.16% AUC but dropping to 76.37% accuracy at threshold 0.5 in `P0036`; `DNF` showing 50% accuracy vs 81.5% AP in `P0037`; naive 80%–85% judicial cutoffs collapsing recall to 18% in `P0031`; and 60% of ensemble errors falling within `<0.5` probability margin in `P0040`). While initial efforts explore OOD abnormality scoring (`P0029`), Packed-Ensembles ECE/Brier reduction (`P0030`), and AURC risk-coverage evaluation (`P0032`), no prior architecture integrates empirical validation calibration, collector reliability, pipeline completeness, and structured four-way abstention (`MOD008`–`MOD009`) inside an auditable forensic system.
