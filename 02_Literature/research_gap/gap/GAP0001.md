# Research Gap: GAP0001 — Generalization Breakdown Across Generative Paradigm Shift (GAN vs. Diffusion Models)

**Category:** Cross-Generator Generalization  
**Supporting Papers:** P0003, P0005, P0006, P0007, P0010, P0016, P0018, P0021, P0023, P0024, P0028  
**Frequency:** 11  
**Conflicting Papers:** None  
**Importance:** Critical  
**Potential Contribution:** Unifying feature representations and multi-expert fusion capable of detecting synthetic facial images regardless of the underlying generative architecture (GAN vs. Latent Diffusion).  
**Implementation Difficulty:** Hard  
**Publication Potential:** Very High  

---

## Overview & Description

Classifiers trained on GAN-generated faces (e.g., StyleGAN, StarGAN) rely heavily on architectural artifacts like high-frequency convolutional traces or specific grid-like upsampling spectral anomalies. Diffusion models generate images through iterative denoising in latent space, producing radically different noise distributions and high-frequency residuals. As a result, state-of-the-art GAN detectors suffer a performance collapse (often dropping to near-chance binary accuracy < 30%) when evaluated on diffusion-generated images such as Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, and DALL-E, and vice-versa.

### Research Opportunity & Forensic Impact
Developing invariant forensic feature representations (or multi-specialist ensemble structures) that generalize across both GAN and diffusion generator families. This is a foundational requirement for robust, real-world forensic deployment where the adversary generative engine is unknown.
