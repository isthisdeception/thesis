# Research Gap: GAP0007 — Absence of Unified Hybrid Spatial-Frequency Feature Fusion Architectures

**Category:** Architecture & Feature Fusion  
**Supporting Papers:** P0003, P0005, P0010, P0015, P0022  
**Frequency:** 5  
**Conflicting Papers:** None  
**Importance:** High  
**Potential Contribution:** Dual-branch hybrid architecture fusing spatial CNN/ViT features with multi-band frequency domain representations (FFT/DCT/wavelet) via dynamic cross-attention.  
**Implementation Difficulty:** Medium  
**Publication Potential:** High  

---

## Overview & Description

Most detectors operate exclusively in either the spatial domain (analyzing pixel patterns, local textures, and semantic details) or the frequency domain (analyzing Fourier or DCT power spectra). Spatial models capture high-level structural anomalies but miss high-frequency spectral artifacts; frequency models capture subtle grid traces but struggle with semantic context. Unified dual-domain architectures with calibrated feature fusion remain under-explored.

### Research Opportunity & Forensic Impact
Designing an integrated dual-branch network that explicitly combines spatial texture features and frequency spectral features for complementary artifact identification.
