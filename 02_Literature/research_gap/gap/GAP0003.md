# Research Gap: GAP0003 — Lack of Interpretable, Multi-Modal Forensic Explanations and Evidence Fusion

**Category:** Explainability & Forensic Analysis  
**Supporting Papers:** P0003, P0005, P0008, P0010, P0015, P0016, P0019, P0020, P0022  
**Frequency:** 9  
**Conflicting Papers:** None  
**Importance:** Critical  
**Potential Contribution:** The AI Forensic Analyst — an evidence-driven system that aggregates, cross-validates, and fuses independent forensic evidence streams (spatial anomalies, spectral residuals, biometric/physiological inconsistencies, and model attribution) into calibrated, human-understandable forensic case reports.  
**Implementation Difficulty:** Hard  
**Publication Potential:** Very High  

---

## Overview & Description

Existing synthetic face detection systems operate predominantly as opaque black-box classifiers outputting a scalar confidence score. In legal and forensic investigations, unexplainable probability scores are inadmissible and unconvincing. There is a lack of unified forensic systems that combine spatial gradient maps, frequency domain spectral anomalies, biometric/anatomical checks (e.g., iris symmetry, pupil shape, facial landmarks), and attribution evidence into an interpretable forensic audit trail.

### Research Opportunity & Forensic Impact
Directly anchors the primary thesis contribution: building a modular AI Forensic Analyst that collects, verifies, and fuses multi-domain forensic evidence streams into explainable, calibrated, audit-ready forensic reports.
